Global Export Markets

Exportrio exports Indian non-basmati and basmati rice to high-volume international markets, with a strong focus on West Africa and containerized FOB India shipments.

Primary Export Markets

🇧🇯 Benin

One of the largest importers of Indian non-basmati rice. Strong demand for IR64, parboiled, and long-grain white rice. Strategic gateway to West Africa.

🇸🇳 Senegal

Major West African rice market with consistent imports of parboiled and non-basmati rice for mass consumption.

🇬🇳 Guinea

Growing rice import volumes with preference for competitively priced Indian non-basmati varieties.

🇹🇬 Togo

Strategic West African port market supporting regional distribution and inland consumption.

🇰🇪 Kenya

Significant importer of IR64 and long-grain rice for retail and institutional consumption.

Secondary & Emerging Markets

Bangladesh

Large-volume non-basmati imports driven by domestic demand.

Nepal

Regional trade partner with consistent rice imports from India.

UAE

Trading hub serving Middle East and Africa.

Saudi Arabia & Oman

Stable Middle Eastern markets with structured import demand.

United States

Growing demand for Indian non-basmati rice varieties.

Vietnam

Imports for blending, processing, and redistribution.

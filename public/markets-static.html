Global Export Markets

Exportrio supplies Indian non-basmati rice to high-volume international markets, with a strategic focus on West Africa and containerized FOB India shipments.

Primary High-Volume Markets

🇧🇯 Benin (Primary Focus)

One of the largest importers of Indian non-basmati rice. Strong demand for IR64, parboiled rice, and long-grain white rice. Strategic gateway to Nigeria and inland West Africa.

🇸🇳 Senegal

Major West African rice-consuming market with consistent demand for parboiled and non-basmati rice.

🇬🇳 Guinea

Growing import volumes for competitively priced Indian non-basmati rice.

🇹🇬 Togo

Strategic port-based market supporting regional distribution across West Africa.

🇰🇪 Kenya

Significant importer of IR64 and long-grain rice for retail and institutional consumption.

Secondary & Emerging Markets

Bangladesh

Large-volume non-basmati imports driven by domestic demand.

Nepal

Strong regional trade links and consistent imports from India.

United Arab Emirates

Trading hub serving Middle East and African markets.

Saudi Arabia & Oman
